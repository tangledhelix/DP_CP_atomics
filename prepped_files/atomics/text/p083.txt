Lawrence took advantage of these well-known facts in the construction
of his cyclotron for obtaining high-speed atomic bullets. Let us see how an
early model was constructed.

He arranged a flat cylindrical metal can, cut right down the middle as
shown in the diagram. He then separated the two halves A and B by an inch
or so and connected them to the
terminals of a source of alternating
voltage producing about 10,000
volts. Using this alternating voltage,
the positive and negative
charges are made to reverse many
times each second so that at one
instant one side of the box is + and
the other -- while an instant later
the situation is reversed. The half
box formerly charged + now becomes
--, and vice versa. We shall
see presently the purpose of this
alternating voltage.

The two half boxes are placed between the poles of a giant magnet. The box
and its container are then connected to a high vacuum pump and most of the
air removed, to permit the particles to move without interference from air
molecules.

And now the "merry-go-round" begins. Some charged hydrogen atoms
(protons) are created by bombarding ordinary hydrogen gas with electrons
(produced by a hot filament). These rip off the planetary electrons to form
hydrogen ions, which in this case happen to be protons. These protons are
formed close to the center of the cylindrical box, between the two halves. The
positive proton is immediately attracted toward whichever half of the box
happens to be negative at that moment. If A happens to be negatively charged
it will move toward A. But at the same time the magnetic field acting on the
particle causes it to swerve in a circular path and thus return to the space
between the two boxes once again.

Now, since the two halves A and B alternate in being the plus and minus
terminals, in the same manner as an ordinary alternating-current house electricity
supply, the following interesting effect takes place. At the very moment
that the proton returns to the center line, the plus and minus terminals alternate.
