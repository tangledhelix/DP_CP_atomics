
Chapter Twenty-six

Atomic War--World Suicide?

Our travels along the paths of atomic adventure have taken us
into the beginning of the Atomic Energy Age. In these travels we
have high-lighted the paths leading to increased plenty and new
weapons for frontal attacks on malignant diseases.

Another path, far more somber in aspect, is now coming into view. Our
new plaything, atomic energy, in a manner similar to fire and dynamite, has
also great potentialities for destruction. Viewed simply as a novel weapon, it
would be expected to have a significant effect on military thought. When,
however, the magnitude of the havoc that may be wrought by this weapon is
considered, the problem is lifted from the textbooks on ordnance and immediately
becomes one of the most pressing problems confronting all of us.  '
