Some may question the calculations deduced from angles and velocities
and ask how it is possible to tell from such evidence what kinds of atoms
are being transmuted. A good test of theory is actual practice. Evidence
such as this was the basis upon which uranium was transmuted into the entirely
new element plutonium in quantities large enough to make an atomic
bomb.

Such final proof is enough to convince many skeptics.
