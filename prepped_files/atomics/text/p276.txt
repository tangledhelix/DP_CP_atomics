Some day in the future when historians write about our time and its problems,
they will record the decisions that we are making concerning the disposition
of atomic energy. What will these decisions be? Will they, as many
fear, be the same old decisions that have plunged our globe into war so many
times in the past, and the next time into oblivion? Or shall we at last grow
up into adulthood and make the only decision which common sense dictates
and which necessity impels us to make--the final and complete elimination
of war?

It depends on us alone what that decision shall be. Our children and the
unborn generations beyond them shall have reason to bless us or to malign us,
according to the outcome of the momentous part that we must play at this
