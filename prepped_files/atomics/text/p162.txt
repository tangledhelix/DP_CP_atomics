"To operate the pile all but one of the cadmium strips were taken out. The
remaining one was then slowly pulled out. As the critical conditions were approached,
the intensity of neutrons emitted by the pile began to increase rapidly....
[It was] relatively easy to keep the pile operating at a constant level
of intensity.

"The pile was first operated on December 2, 1942, to a maximum energy
production of about 1/2 watt. On December 12th the intensity was run up to
about 200 watts, but it was not felt safe to go higher because of the danger
of the radiation to personnel in and around the building."

It is clear from the facts above that the Atomic Energy Age (if a date of
inception has to be given) might be dated almost 2-1/2 years earlier than the
first atomic bomb, namely, December 2, 1942. For on that date the first self-sustaining
atomic-energy machine in human history was actually operated.
