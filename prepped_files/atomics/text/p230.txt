Another area of development wherein the advantage of the atomic-energy 1

With an atomic engine,

When the lid was lifted on atomic-energy secrecy in 1945, many adventurous
science writers contributed to the popular impression that cars, trucks,
small boats, and the home would be powered by small uranium piles. Nothing
could be further from reality. In the first place, too much precious fissionable
material must be tied up in one of these units in order to reach chain-reacting
size. But an even more important reason is the dangerous radioactivity, the
emission of neutrons and gamma rays, that accompanies the operation of a
pile. This necessitates the erection of cumbersome radiation shields and the
presence of skilled personnel.
