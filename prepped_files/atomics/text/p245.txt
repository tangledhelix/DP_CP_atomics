But let us now return to more practical matters of the moment. Aside from
the question of energy and power, there are certain extremely valuable indirect
uses of atomic energy which can be applied immediately to help us
in many ways. The great variety of new isotopes of common elements, many
of which are radioactive, provide us with a bountiful storehouse of ace
detectives for a host of chemical and biological studies. Out of these experiments
in "tagging the atom" will come great treasure in the form of better
products for our use and longer life for ourselves and our children. Let us
see how this will be achieved.
