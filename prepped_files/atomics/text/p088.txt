Another device that shows great promise is the linear accelerator, which
uses a series of separate and properly timed pushes from a large number of
high-voltage sources arranged in a straight line. A number of these are now
being developed, with good prospects for the production of particles with energies
of several hundred million electron volts. Possibilities also exist for the
production of billion electron volt particles with this machine at some time
in the future.

It should be quite apparent to the reader that atomic science is on the
threshold of important discoveries arising from this new crop of atom smashers.
Developments should be very rapid from this point on as we probe the
depths of the nucleus itself with these machines. The next decade should
eclipse by far all the wonderful discoveries achieved by our early atom
smashing machines.
