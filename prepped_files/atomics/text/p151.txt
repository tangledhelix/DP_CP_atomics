At the end of the twenty-eighth round over 134 million people--practically
the entire population of the United States--are required to keep the
chain doubling at the same rate. Thus one person can soon involve the
entire nation in letter writing by the simple process of getting two others
to write letters. And it only takes 28 rounds in the chain to do it. The
growth is enormous. This example illustrates a k factor of 2.

The explosive nature of growth with k equal to 2 is also shown in a
startling manner with an oft-told problem. Let us suppose the reader is
offered a choice of one of two similar jobs. One job pays $100,000 a day for
a period of 30 days. The other pays only 1 cent the first day, 2 cents the
next, 4 cents the next, 8 cents the next, and so on, doubling each day for
30 days. Which job would you take?
