Now, to come back to the case of table salt. We know today that an atom of
sodium is lighter than an atom of chlorine; in fact, the two weights are to each
other as 23 is to 35-1/2. Also, 1 atom of sodium and 1 atom of chlorine are always
found in a molecule of sodium chloride. Therefore, whether we have 1 molecule,
1,000 molecules, or a whole mountain of table salt, the ratio of sodium to
chlorine by weight will always be 23:35-1/2.

What do the atoms weigh? . . .
