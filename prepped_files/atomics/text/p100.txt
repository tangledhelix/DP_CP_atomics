
The unlimited supply of energy that lay all about us in the form of ordinary
matter intrigued the scientists, as well as many writers of fiction. But how could
we obtain this energy, locked so securely inside the atoms of common materials?
Research in nuclear reactions was intensified. A clue was soon discovered
in the form of a new particle, the neutron. With the discovery of this particle
a new path was to be opened that led within a brief 10 years to the actual
production of a large-scale atomic-energy machine.
