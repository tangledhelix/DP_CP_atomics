of the molecules making up the orderly cell structure and thus to destroy the
tissue. Their use in the treatment of malignant growths will be described in
Chapter Twenty-five.

This important property of the gamma rays was not fully understood by
some early researchers in this field. Some of them suffered the consequences
in severe radium burns and cancers. Today elaborate precautions are taken
by scientific workers to protect themselves from injury, with the installation
of thick lead sheets and plenty of distance between the investigator and the
radioactive material.

The discovery of the radiations and high-speed particles emitted by radium
proved to be one of the most powerful stimuli to research that science has
ever known. Almost every scientist sensed that this new discovery would
lead to unusual results. Here was a phenomenon that could be most fruitful
in discovering the internal architecture of the atom. Perhaps it might be
possible to capture or control some of the inexhaustible energy which the
radium possessed. Many scientists now took up the hunt for the solution to
the mysteries presented by these radiations.

Alchemy in nature . . .

Bubble a slow current of air into a solution of a radium salt and examine
the air leaving the solution. It is also radioactive! Now if we remove all the
air from this gas, a very small amount is left which is highly radioactive.
This gas is not radium, but a different element, which we call radon.

Radon has an atomic weight of 222 and belongs to the family of gases which
are chemically inert. Helium is the lightest member of this family. It was
soon discovered that alpha particles are the only high-energy particles emitted
by radium atoms. The alphas rapidly rob the surrounding molecules of electrons
and end up simply as helium atoms. Thus we are able to write a simple
equation expressing one of nature's transmutations of one element into
another, an effort which had so engaged the attention of the early alchemists
and which had been crowned by so dismal a failure.

Radium --> Radon + Helium
(atomic weight 226) = (atomic weight 222) -+- (atomic weight 4)
