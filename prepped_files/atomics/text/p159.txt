water has been in operation at Chalk River in Canada.

It is interesting to note that many common materials containing carbon
and hydrogen, such as wood, cloth, paper, water, and wax are good "slow-
downers." However, the ordinary hydrogen within them captures too many
neutrons and thus renders them useless for neutron slow-down purposes
in an atomic-energy machine.

SLOW DOWNER

Now the question arises as to how to arrange our moderator material so
as to accomplish the task of slowing down neutrons in a pile. Mixing the
particles of the moderator and the uranium intimately is of little help.
Suppose that a fast neutron collides with three successive carbon atoms,
losing speed from 1,000 miles per second to 500 miles per second, and then
on the fourth collision strikes a U238 atom. The neutron may be captured,
inasmuch as it has not been slowed down sufficiently to prevent its capture
by U238.
