What happens when a positron collides with an electron? It has now been
established that the electron-positron pair becomes completely annihilated and
in the place of the vanished matter there appears a penetrating gamma ray, the
energy of which can be calculated by the Einstein relation. In many previous
illustrations of the conversion of matter into energy, only a portion of the mass
of the particles involved was transformed into energy. But in this case all the
mass of the electron and positron is converted into highly penetrating gamma
radiation.
