The hunt now shifts to Cambridge, England . . .

In Rutherford's laboratory at Cambridge, England, where hunting expeditions
for the neutron had been organized before, the possibility that these
radiations were neutrons was examined by Chadwick. From the cloud-chamber
tracks of the protons given off from this mysterious radiation, Chadwick
was able to estimate the weight (mass) and speed of the particles causing
the protons to be thrown out of the paraffin (on the assumption that the effect
was caused by moving particles). His calculations showed that the weight
was approximately 1, the same as that of the neutron he sought. The fact that
the radiation passed so easily through lead and other materials was exactly
the property that Rutherford had predicted for such an uncharged particle.
The fact that it did not leave a visible track in the cloud chamber was another
property that had been surmised in advance.
