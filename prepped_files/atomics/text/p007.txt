Very unscientific, indeed! But not much further from the truth than
the idea of the ancient Greeks that all matter was made of the four
elements : earth, water, air, and fire.
From earliest times mankind has wondered what things were made of,
has probed to determine what are the building blocks of the universe. The
answer to this puzzle carries with it the power to make healing drugs, beautiful
colors, and sturdy metals. Our giant industries today bear testimony to
the fact that we have discovered some of these secrets and put them to use.

The tiny atom commands an important place in this industrial picture. It
now occupies an even more prominent part of our attention, because of its key
role in the culminating achievement of science, the release of atomic energy.
