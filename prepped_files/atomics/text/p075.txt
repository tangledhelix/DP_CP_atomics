Designing a nucleus--a pleasant game . . .

Here, then, are the rules as we follow them today. Inside the nucleus are
protons and neutrons. Electrons are not considered to be fundamental building
blocks inside the nucleus or core of the atom. The weight of each particle
in the nucleus is very close to i. Start with hydrogen of atomic number I,
atomic weight i. Here as before, the nucleus is simply a proton. Now let us
build up the nucleus of helium, atomic number 2 and atomic weight 4. After
a bit of doodling, it is easy to see that there is only one way to do this : 2 protons
and 2 neutrons. The 2 protons furnish the charge of +2 and the 4 particles,
each of weight 1, give a total atomic weight of 4.
