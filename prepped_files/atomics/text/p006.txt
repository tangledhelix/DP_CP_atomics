Our discovery of a way to tap even a small portion of this almost unlimited
amount of energy carries with it the greatest potentialities, for good as well
as for harm. When the energy is released slowly and under our control, it
can be harnessed in a great many ways. But when it is deliberately allowed to
be released suddenly it can destroy large regions. It is in the hands of all of
us to decide what these uses shall be. For this reason we have sketched in this
book the fascinating story leading to this rich treasure for which our generation
is the first trustee.

Atomic story--a real "whodunit" . . .

The story of atomic energy is a blend of the mystery novel and a saga of
exploration. Here is the tale of how the secrets of the atom have been probed.
Many obvious clues turned out to be false leads; many of the solutions proved
to be remarkably simple. Here we find investigators who could hardly be
surpassed by the master sleuths of fiction.

Our story is also an account of discovery. Although dealing with the things
that make up the universe around us, we start on trails that 1,000 years ago
were completely uncharted. Scarcely any light fell on these trails until very
recently. But about 50 years ago the dramatic discovery of radioactivity pointed
the way toward the desired goals. Now, with the release of large-scale atomic
energy, we may make even more exciting strides forward.

Let us return to the earliest of these trails leading to atomic energy, to the
Greeks who over 2,000 years ago first speculated about the very existence of
the atom.
