
Very fast neutrons are also produced in the collision of deuterons with
lithium atoms. When 5-M.e.v. deuterons are used, the neutrons produced
range in energy up to 20 M.e.v. The nuclear event is written :

Deuteron + lithium = helium + helium + neutron

These processes were used in order to obtain high-energy neutron sources--effective
in unraveling many secrets of nuclear particles and forces. The deuteron
will be a useful atomic projectile as long as cyclotrons are used in
producing high-energy beams of this material.

Another important particle, the positron . . .

Thus far our list of important particles includes the proton, electron, neutron,
deuteron, and the alpha particle (or helium nucleus). Still another fundamental
particle, the positron, was discovered at about the same time that the
neutron and the deuteron were found. But the way in which it was found,
through a study of the cosmic rays, is an important story in itself. We shall
turn now to a consideration of cosmic rays and to the discovery of this new
atomic particle.
