In the cylinder of your automobile the gasoline-air mixture is exploding at a
"slow" rate and as a result the piston is moving while the reaction is going on.

cylinder would probably be ruined in a very short time. In this case we do not
want too rapid an explosion. But when we use high explosives, the opposite
is true. In fact, when we talk about explosion intervals, we find it hard to
