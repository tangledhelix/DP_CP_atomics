* General notes for later PP phase
- Chapters are divided into sections. Use "4-dots" rule on those section headings.
- MULTIPLICATION SIGN (U+00D7) [part of Basic Latin character suite]
  - pp. 96, 99, 117
- RIGHTWARDS ARROW (U+2192) [enabled as custom character]
  - pp. 61, 90, 93-95, 99, 105, 107-108, 111, 128, 243
- Horizontal curly braces (grouping items in a table)
  - pp. 94, 105, 111, 128
- Poetry used as chapter subtitle?
  - p. 7
- h3 immediately follows h2
  - pp. 53, 66, 89, 101
- =, ✕ printed larger in the paper edition - increase size with <span>?
- Ratios, e.g. 23:35-1/2 - does fraction tool understand this?
- Comma in fraction: 1/1,000 - does fraction tool understand this?
- Section headings sometimes end ~?...~ sometimes ~?....~; standardize
- Section headings sometimes end ~...~ sometimes ~....~; standardize
- A lot of proofers are treating U235, U238 as having subscripts, which is not correct. ~U_{235}~ would be a molecule made of 235 uranium atoms, which is not what U235 is.
- Other chemical notations /do/ need subscripts, however, such as ~H_{2}O~.
