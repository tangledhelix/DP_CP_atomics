# About the book

Maurice Sendak was a beloved author and illustrator who probably needs no introduction. ([But in case you need one....][5])

Before his illustrious career, Sendak was just another teenager at a Brooklyn high school. Atomics for the Millions, co-written by his physics teacher, Hyman Ruchlis, and Dr. Maxwell Leigh Eidinoff, a local college professor and Manhattan Project alumnus, contains the first published illustrations by Sendak. Today it is a collector's item that commands high prices on the used market, and many library catalogs list it as "library use only."

Legend has it that Sendak was paid not only in royalties but also with a passing grade in Ruchlis's physics class.
